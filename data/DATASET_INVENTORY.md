## Quarantined Synthetic Data Policy

In strict compliance with **Task Tracker Principle 2.5 ("No Fabricated Data")** and **Phase 1 Rule 1, Rule 4, and Rule 9**:
1. Synthetic artifacts have been quarantined in `data/quarantined_synthetic/`.
2. All operational files under `data/processed/` (`interactions.csv`, `products.csv`, `users.csv`, `popular_products.csv`) are 100% authentic, verified, and derived from `data/raw/train_part1.csv` and `data/raw/train_part2.csv`.
3. No machine learning models shall ever access or consume the quarantined synthetic files.
