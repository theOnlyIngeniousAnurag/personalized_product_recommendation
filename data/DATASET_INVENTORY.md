## Quarantined Synthetic Data Policy

In strict compliance with **Task Tracker Principle 2.5 ("No Fabricated Data")** and **Phase 1 Rule 1 & Rule 4**:
1. `data/processed/interactions.csv` and `data/processed/products.csv` are explicitly quarantined.
2. They are retained in the repository purely as historical artifacts to avoid destructive deletion, but are designated as:
   `SYNTHETIC — NOT FOR TRAINING/EVALUATION`.
3. No machine learning models (Collaborative Filtering, Matrix Factorization, Content-Based, Hybrid) shall be trained on them.
4. No quantitative evaluation metrics (Precision@K, Recall@K, NDCG@K, RMSE) shall be reported from them.
